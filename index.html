LeanRoll Support

LeanRoll finds duplicate photos, similar shots, screenshots and large videos, and lets you swipe through a month of photos to decide what to keep. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Common questions

Will LeanRoll delete anything by itself? No. You mark items, review them, confirm, and iOS asks once more before anything is removed.

Can I get a deleted photo back? Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for a limited time. Open Photos, go to Recently Deleted and choose Recover.

What does "Keep Best" do? In each group it keeps one photo (a favorite if there is one, otherwise the highest resolution) and marks the rest.

Too many or too few similar photos? Change Settings → Similar photo matching to Strict or Loose.

Why are some photos missing from the scan? If you gave access to selected photos only, only those are scanned. Change this in the Settings app under LeanRoll → Photos.

Why is "space freed" approximate? Photo sizes are estimated from their resolution. Video sizes are measured when the file is stored on the device.

LeanRoll Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026.

LeanRoll helps you find and remove duplicate photos, similar shots, screenshots and large videos on your iPhone. It works on your device and does not collect any data about you.

Your photos and videos

With your permission, LeanRoll reads your photo library to compare pictures and list items you may want to remove. This analysis runs on your iPhone. Your photos, videos and the results of the analysis are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. LeanRoll has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software that sends data off your device. The app does not make network requests.

What is stored on your device

Your settings, your progress numbers (items deleted and approximate space freed) and a small cache of picture fingerprints used to speed up the next scan. Removing the app removes all of it.

Deleting photos

Nothing is deleted until you review your selection and confirm, and iOS asks you to confirm again. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for a limited time.

Permissions

Photos: required to scan your library and to delete the items you choose. You can give access to selected photos only, or turn access off at any time in the Settings app.

Children

LeanRoll does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
